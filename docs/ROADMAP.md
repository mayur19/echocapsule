# Delivery roadmap

1. **Foundation:** lifecycle, encryption, content commitments, manifest and reveal verification.
2. **Collecting:** accounts, invitations, prompts, revisioned cards, media and participant privacy.
3. **Waiting and opening:** durable scheduler, reminders, reveal gallery and hosted sequence.
4. **Keepsake:** predictions, future replies, archive and verified export.
5. **Public readiness:** moderation, cancellation, key operations, accessibility, deployment and browser coverage.
